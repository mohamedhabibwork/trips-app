How deliveries work

Every request is signed. Check the X-TE-Signature header (t=…,v1=…) with your endpoint's secret. Anything other than 2xx is retried 8 times over 24 hours with growing gaps. After 3 days of failures the endpoint is disabled and the owner is emailed.
