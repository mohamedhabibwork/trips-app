In short: only a child’s own parent or guardian can follow them live. Bus staff see only the children on their bus, and only for that trip. Live locations are deleted after 30 days.

This section explains how Trips Enjoy handles information about children under 18 who travel on trips booked through our platform, including school trips arranged by an organizer. It follows the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and applies in addition to our Privacy policy.

What we collect about a child

  • First name, last name and age, added by a parent or by the school when it books the trip.
  • The trip, bus and seat the child is booked on.
  • Boarding events: each check-in and check-out with the time, the bus’s GPS position and the moderator who recorded it.
  • The bus’s live location while the trip is in progress. We never track a child’s own phone.

We do not collect a child’s phone number, email address or photo for tracking, and we never use child data for advertising or marketing.

Consent

A parent or legal guardian gives consent when they add a child to their family or link a child to a trip with a booking reference or school code. For school trips, the school confirms it holds each parent’s permission, and live tracking starts only for parents who have linked their child.

You can withdraw consent at any time in the app under Family, or by emailing our Data Protection Officer. Withdrawing stops live tracking for future trips; the boarding log for past trips is kept for the period above because the organizer needs it for safety records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your child’s data, or get a copy of it. Email dpo@trips-enjoy.com from the address on your account. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).
